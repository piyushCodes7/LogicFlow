💡 Learning Notes

🔵 Forward diodes in series add voltage drops (~0.7V each). More diodes = more total Vf required.

🔴 One reverse diode in a series chain completely blocks current — the chain is broken.

⚡ Standard circuits use single diodes for predictable 0.7V forward drop and full control.
